Write operations, programs, verification reports and stock.

Actions
- create: add an operation.
- update: change settings; invalidate NC.
- generate: store NC; return stats and preview.
- verify: replay the stored program (never regenerated) and store stock-removal, collision and gouge findings bound to its programHash; generate first if none.
- stock_set: change stock; invalidate programs.
- manufacture: kernel route for a whole part: store its multi-tool NC (M98 subprograms) and release evidence. Preview with cam manufacture first; read evidence with cam review.

Parameters (mm, mm/min, rpm, angles in degrees)
- create: type (contour, pocket, drill, engrave, profile, threading, 3d_surface, probing, multiaxis, facing, turning_od, parting_od, ...), params - the generator settings: toolType, toolDiameter, depth, stepdown, feedrate, rpm, material (aluminum, steel, wood, plastic, brass, titanium, composite, other), offset (inside|outside|center), workOffset (G54..G59), originType, machineType, and printer extras (infillDensity, supportType, shellCount).
- update: operationId, params (changed fields only).
- generate: operationId (required; list them with cam operations), maxLines (1-200, default 40).
- verify: operationId, tool {cuttingLength, shankDiameter, stickout, holderDiameter, holderLength}, fixtures [{id, bounds:{minX,maxX,minY,maxY,minZ,maxZ}}], targetSolids, stock (same bounds), resolution, tolerance. Omitted fields are not modelled.
- stock_set: stock {width, height, depth, radius, x, y, topZ, allowance, material?, elementId?}.
- manufacture: as cam manufacture (material, machine, origin, setupOrientation, finishAllowance, scallop, deburr, operations, tools, subprograms) plus expectedRevision. Blocked releases are not saved.

Result (JSON)
- ok; create: operationId; generate: programHash, lines/returned/omitted, preview head (the stored program is complete); verify: status, programHash, issues, stats, coverage (resolution, omitted issues), modelling limits; stock_set: stock/workpiece, invalidated operations.
- manufacture: operationId, release, NC identity. Desktop loads the original; cam manufacture previews without writing.

Do not use it
- For machine/operation reads or static analysis: use cam.
- To claim a program is safe: verify reports only what it modelled.

After it
- Send expectedRevision on create/update; stale plans require fresh plan_check.
- verify: start {x,y,z} is the initial tip; otherwise X0 Y0 at stock top + 10 mm is assumed.
- Read cam review after verification. At one drawing/stock revision, allow at most three refinements; stop at refinement_limit and report the cause.
