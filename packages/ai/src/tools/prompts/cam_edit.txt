Write operations, programs, verification reports and stock.

Actions
- create: add an operation from type and params.
- update: change params and invalidate stored G-code.
- generate: store generated G-code; return stats and a line preview.
- verify: simulate the operation's program against stock; store removal/collision/gouge findings and modelling limits.
- stock_set: update raw stock/workpiece and invalidate affected programs.

Parameters (mm, mm/min, rpm, angles in degrees)
- create: type (contour, pocket, drill, engrave, profile, threading, 3d_surface, probing, multiaxis, facing, turning_od, parting_od, ...), params - the generator settings: toolType, toolDiameter, depth, stepdown, feedrate, rpm, material (aluminum, steel, wood, plastic, brass, titanium, composite, other), offset (inside|outside|center), workOffset (G54..G59), originType, machineType, and printer extras (infillDensity, supportType, shellCount).
- update: operationId, params - only the fields that change; the rest of the stored settings are kept.
- generate: operationId (the newest operation when omitted), maxLines (1-200, default 40).
- verify: operationId, tool {cuttingLength, shankDiameter, stickout, holderDiameter, holderLength}, fixtures [{id, bounds:{minX,maxX,minY,maxY,minZ,maxZ}}], targetSolids, stock (same bounds shape), resolution, tolerance. Give what you know: a field you leave out was not modelled.
- stock_set: stock {width, height, depth, radius, x, y, topZ, allowance, material?, elementId?}.

Result (JSON)
- ok; create returns operationId and geometry; generate stats and preview; verify issues/codes, stats and modelled fields; stock_set stock, workpiece and invalidated operations.
- ok:false reports an empty drawing, missing operation or refused generation.

Do not use it
- To read the machine, the operations or a static analysis: cam does that without writing.
- To claim a program is safe: verify reports only what it modelled.

After it
- For a checked plan, send expectedRevision on create/update. A stale refusal requires fresh plan_check.
- verify accepts start {x,y,z} for the actual initial tool tip. Without it, replay assumes X0 Y0 at stock top + 10 mm and reports that assumption.
- Read cam review after verification. Failed operations have at most three refinements at one drawing/stock revision; stop at refinement_limit and report the unresolved cause.
- Generate, analyse, verify, fix by cause, regenerate and verify again. After three rounds on the same finding, report the blocker.
