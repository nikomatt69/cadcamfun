Read machining data. All actions are read-only.

Actions
- plan_check: validate proposed operations (drawing, stock, schemas, DFM, machine limits, generation); a pass validates a plan, not a part.
- review: saved pass/failed/stale/unverified evidence and refinements per drawing/program revision; stale or unverified cannot accept a program.
- machine: axes, zeros, limits, accepted operations; read first.
- operations: drawing operations, settings, program availability.
- suggest: depths, speeds, chip load and warnings from supplied settings.
- compare_setups: generate and verify candidates on the stock.
- analyze: static G-code and machine-axis findings, not a safety verdict.
- stock: configured stock, volume and workpieceMatchesStock.
- vision: the displayed CAM preview and its evidence.
- chain_suggest: next operation.
- chain_status: previous operation, rest tool, warnings.
- materials, cutting_data, manufacture: the native CAM kernel. Grades; feeds/speeds of a cutter in a material; manufacture previews the whole-part plan read from the exact B-rep (holes, pockets, outlines as features, else a 3D surface job), its NC and its replay and clearance claims. planOnly returns the plan without NC.

Parameters (mm, mm/min, rpm)
- machine: machineType (mill|lathe|3dprinter).
- operations, stock: none.
- plan_check: operations (1-8 of {type, params}), expectedRevision (optional drawing revision). Use the returned revision on create/update.
- review: operationId.
- suggest: params (operationType, toolDiameter, depth, stepdown, feedrate, rpm, material, ...).
- compare_setups: candidates (1-4 of {name, params, toolId?, materialId?}) from libraries.
- analyze: operationId (full stored program; previews are truncated) or gcode; machineType adds axis checks.
- vision: focus.
- chain_suggest: fromOperationId, materialHint, toolHint.
- chain_status: operationId.
- materials: query. cutting_data: material, cut, tool, machine, depth/ae/ap/scallop. manufacture: material, machine, origin, setupOrientation (+Z default|-Z|+X|-X|+Y|-Y: the CAD side facing the spindle after a manual refixture; one program and workOffset per setup), finishAllowance, scallop, deburr, planOnly, operations, tools, subprograms (M98 for repeated toolpaths).

Result (JSON)
- ok and data/findings. Empty analyze issues means "no static findings", never "safe".

Do not use it
- For writes or stored verification: use cam_edit.
- For geometry: use cad.

After it
- Create/update through cam_edit with the checked revision; generate, analyze the full program, verify and review current evidence.
