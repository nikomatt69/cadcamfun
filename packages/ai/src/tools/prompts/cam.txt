Read CAM data: machine, stock, toolpaths, G-code, materials, cutting data and evidence; read-only.
Call with action and its fields; previews do not persist programs.

Actions and Parameters (mm, mm/min, rpm)
- machine: machineType (mill|lathe|3dprinter), gcode or operationId, params, at (seconds). Viewer timeline, tooling, B/C poses, limits.
- operations, stock: no parameters; saved operations or configured stock.
- plan_check: operations (1-8 of {type, params}), expectedRevision. A pass checks the plan, not the machined part.
- regenerate: operationId, scope program|drawing; preview of a fresh program with diff and safe flag; stores nothing.
- setup_sheet: operationId; tools, operations, times of a saved kernel program.
- review: operationId; pass/failed/stale/unverified/preview evidence for the saved drawing and NC; modelChange when stale.
- suggest: params (operationType, toolDiameter, depth, stepdown, feedrate, rpm, material).
- compare_setups: candidates (1-4 of {name, params, toolId?, materialId?}).
- analyze: operationId (full stored NC) or gcode; machineType adds axis checks. Empty findings never mean safe.
- vision: focus; viewport/toolpath image and line-numbered program sheet (sent whenever the editor shows code).
- chain_suggest: fromOperationId, materialHint, toolHint.
- chain_status: operationId.
- materials: query; cutting grades and FFF filaments (densityGcm3, nozzleTemperature, bedTemperature, fan, maxVolumetricFlow, qualification basis). Machines: kind, post, limits, fiveAxis/turning/printer profiles (printers: bed, buildHeight, nozzle, thermal/speed limits) and saved config:<id> rows.
- cutting_data: material, cut, tool, machine, depth/ae/ap/scallop; material-based feeds bounded by machine limits.
- lathe: stock, contour|bore, tools, operations, material, post; NC, stock replay.
- process: gcode|operationId, tool, material, machine, rpm, stock, mode engagement|chip|power, post; load per move, optimised feeds.
- post_test: post, gcode?, expected?; replay checks.
- verify_nc: gcode|operationId, part {elements|meshes}, origin (work zero), stock {minX..maxZ} (work frame), tools [{number,diameter,flutes,kind?,holderDiameter?,holderLength?}], machine, tolerance, allowance, cell; replays foreign NC on the tri-dexel stock: gouge with NC line, rapids in stock, travel. clean|faults|incomplete; clean is not controller acceptance.
- manufacture: material, machine, machineConfig, origin, setupOrientation (+Z|-Z|+X|-X|+Y|-Y), finishAllowance, scallop, deburr, tabs {count,width,height}, breadth {holeCycles,tilted,namedFeatures}, operations, tools, subprograms, nativeCycles, tcp (five-axis machine, post with a tip-control word; else refused), smoothing (mm contour tolerance), postDefinitionId (library post) or postDefinition, planOnly. A cone or cylinder wall is flank-cut on a 5-axis machine by an operation {strategy:multiaxis, mode:swarf, near:[a point on the wall]} (cad exact lists swarfWalls with their near points; plan the roughing first). Library machineConfig (workVolume, maxSpindleSpeed, maxFeedRate, controller, axes, rotary) wins over machine. Manual setups use workOffset, no invented rotary motion. Printers (printer-* or printer machineConfig): material=filament; print sets layerHeight, walls, infillDensity, infillPattern, supports, adhesion, vase, printSpeed, nozzleTemperature, bedTemperature, fan, orientation (auto|as-designed).
- job: op plan|run, material, machine, part {elements|meshes}, tools, quality {resolution,tolerance,enforceTolerance,...}, setups (1-6 of {id,orientation,workOffset,stock,stockModel,fixtures,operations}), nc (lines). Read release, failed/unresolved claims and reasons, coverage, tolerance terms (skill cam-five-axis).
- turn: profile (2-200 of {diameter,z}), stock {diameter,length}, stepdown, allowance, feedMmRev, rpm, machine, machineConfig. Z0 at face, negative towards chuck, X diameter. External rough/finish, stock profiles, G18/G94/G97 NC; machine bounds bar and spindle. stock = part + unmachined tail + removed. Point-tool preview; save via cam_edit turn. Mill-turn uses B head and positioned C spindle.

Result (JSON)
- manufacture release/claims concern only modelled NC capabilities; turn is always preview.
- Printing: report actual print layers/size/filamentGrams/timeSeconds/orientation/settings, release and failed/unresolved claims. ok means analysis completed; retain blocked/preview verdicts. Macros, heater setpoints and comments do not prove homing, thermal readiness or deposition. Explain refusals; use filament data, never claim physical certification from replay.

Do not use it
- For writes or stored verification: use cam_edit. For geometry: use cad.

After it
- Use the matching route: manufacture/turn previews precede their matching cam_edit action; legacy operations use plan_check, create/update, generate, analyze full NC, verify and review. Reuse evidence only while drawing, stock, settings and program identity match.
