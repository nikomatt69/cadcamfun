Read CAM data: machine, stock, toolpaths, G-code, materials, cutting data and evidence; read-only.

Actions and Parameters (mm, mm/min, rpm)
- machine: machineType (mill|lathe|3dprinter), gcode or operationId, params, at (seconds). Shared viewer timeline, tooling, B/C poses and limits.
- operations, stock: no parameters; saved operations or configured stock.
- plan_check: operations (1-8 of {type, params}), expectedRevision. A pass checks the plan, not the machined part.
- setup_sheet: operationId; tools, operations, times of a saved kernel program.
- review: operationId; pass/failed/stale/unverified/preview evidence for the saved drawing and NC.
- suggest: params (operationType, toolDiameter, depth, stepdown, feedrate, rpm, material).
- compare_setups: candidates (1-4 of {name, params, toolId?, materialId?}).
- analyze: operationId (full stored NC) or gcode; machineType adds axis checks. Empty findings never mean safe.
- vision: focus; the current viewport/toolpath image and program sheet (line-numbered G-code and toolpaths). The sheet arrives whenever the editor shows code.
- chain_suggest: fromOperationId, materialHint, toolHint.
- chain_status: operationId.
- materials: query; cutting grades and FFF filaments (densityGcm3, nozzleTemperature, bedTemperature, fan, maxVolumetricFlow, qualification basis). Machines: kind, post, limits, fiveAxis/turning/printer profiles and saved config:<id> rows. Printers include bed, buildHeight, nozzle and thermal/speed limits.
- cutting_data: material, cut, tool, machine, depth/ae/ap/scallop; material-based feeds bounded by machine limits.
- manufacture: material, machine, machineConfig, origin, setupOrientation (+Z|-Z|+X|-X|+Y|-Y), finishAllowance, scallop, deburr, tabs {count,width,height}, operations, tools, subprograms, nativeCycles, smoothing (mm, high-speed contour tolerance the post writes), postDefinitionId (library post) or postDefinition, planOnly. A cone or cylinder wall is flank-cut on a 5-axis machine by an operation {strategy:multiaxis, mode:swarf, near:[a point on the wall]} (cad exact lists swarfWalls with their near points; plan the roughing first). Whole-part NC and replay claims. Library machineConfig (workVolume, maxSpindleSpeed, maxFeedRate, controller, axes, rotary) wins over machine. Manual setups use workOffset, never invented rotary motion. Printers (printer-* or printer machineConfig): material=filament; print sets layerHeight, walls, infillDensity, infillPattern, supports, adhesion, vase, printSpeed, nozzleTemperature, bedTemperature, fan, orientation (auto|as-designed).
- turn: profile (2-200 of {diameter,z}), stock {diameter,length}, stepdown, allowance, feedMmRev, rpm, machine, machineConfig. Z0 at face, negative towards chuck, X diameter. External rough/finish, stock profiles, G18/G94/G97 NC; machine bounds bar and spindle. stock = part + unmachined tail + removed. Point-tool preview; save via cam_edit turn. Mill-turn uses B head and positioned C spindle.

Result (JSON)
- ok and data/findings. manufacture release/claims concern only modelled NC capabilities; turn always returns release preview.
- Printing: report actual print layers/size/filamentGrams/timeSeconds/orientation/settings, release and failed/unresolved claims. ok means analysis completed; retain blocked/preview verdicts. Macros, heater setpoints and comments do not prove homing, thermal readiness or deposition. Explain returned refusals; use filament data and never claim physical certification from replay.

Do not use it
- For writes or stored verification: use cam_edit. For geometry: use cad.

After it
- Create/update with the checked revision, then generate, analyze full NC, verify and review evidence.
