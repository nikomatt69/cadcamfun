Read machining data. All actions are read-only.

Actions
- plan_check: validate proposed operations against drawing, stock, schemas, DFM, machine limits and generation. Pass validates a plan, not a machined part.
- review: saved pass/failed/stale/unverified evidence and remaining refinements, tied to drawing/program revisions. Stale or unverified evidence cannot accept a program.
- machine: axes, zeros, limits and accepted operations; read before programming.
- operations: active drawing operations, settings and program availability.
- suggest: depths, speeds, chip load and warnings from supplied settings.
- compare_setups: generate and verify candidates against configured stock.
- analyze: static G-code and machine-axis findings, not a safety verdict.
- stock: configured stock, volume and workpieceMatchesStock.
- vision: displayed CAM preview with operation and verification evidence.
- chain_suggest: recommend the next operation.
- chain_status: preceding operation, rest-tool reference and warnings.

Parameters (mm, mm/min, rpm)
- machine: machineType (mill|lathe|3dprinter).
- operations, stock: none.
- plan_check: operations (1-8 of {type, params}), expectedRevision (optional drawing revision). Use the returned revision on create/update.
- review: operationId.
- suggest: params (operationType, toolDiameter, depth, stepdown, feedrate, rpm, material, offset, workOffset, ...).
- compare_setups: candidates (1-4 of {name, params, toolId?, materialId?}) from libraries.
- analyze: operationId (checks the full stored program) or gcode; machineType adds axis checks. Generated previews are truncated.
- vision: focus.
- chain_suggest: fromOperationId, materialHint, toolHint.
- chain_status: operationId.

Result (JSON)
- ok and action data/findings. Empty analyze issues means "no static findings", never "safe". Review reports modelling limits and revision validity.

Do not use it
- For writes or stored verification: use cam_edit.
- For geometry: use cad.

After it
- Create/update through cam_edit with the checked revision; generate, analyze the full program, verify and review current evidence.
