Read machining data, plans and saved evidence; all actions are read-only.

Actions and Parameters (mm, mm/min, rpm)
- machine: machineType (mill|lathe|3dprinter), gcode or operationId, params, at (seconds). Shared viewer timeline, tooling, B/C poses and limits.
- operations, stock: no parameters; saved operations or configured stock.
- plan_check: operations (1-8 of {type, params}), expectedRevision. A pass checks the plan, not the machined part.
- review: operationId; pass/failed/stale/unverified/preview evidence for the saved drawing and NC.
- suggest: params (operationType, toolDiameter, depth, stepdown, feedrate, rpm, material).
- compare_setups: candidates (1-4 of {name, params, toolId?, materialId?}).
- analyze: operationId (full stored NC) or gcode; machineType adds axis checks. Empty findings never mean safe.
- vision: focus; the CAM as it is on screen: the viewport image with the rendered toolpath, and the program sheet (the G-code with its line numbers and the toolpaths it holds). The sheet is what arrives while the editor shows the G-code instead of the preview; the program's text is in the result.
- chain_suggest: fromOperationId, materialHint, toolHint.
- chain_status: operationId.
- materials: query; grades, and the machines: the built-in catalogue (milling centres and turning centres, with kind, post, limits, fiveAxis and turning) plus the machine configurations of the library (id config:<id>). Pick the machine from that list, never invent one.
- cutting_data: material, cut, tool, machine, depth/ae/ap/scallop; material-based feeds bounded by machine limits.
- manufacture: material, machine, machineConfig, origin, setupOrientation (+Z|-Z|+X|-X|+Y|-Y), finishAllowance, scallop, deburr, operations, tools, subprograms, planOnly. Exact-B-rep whole-part plan, multi-tool NC and replay claims; planOnly omits NC. machineConfig (a row of the library: workVolume, maxSpindleSpeed, maxFeedRate, controller, axes, rotary) is the machine the person configured and wins over the preset id. Each manual setup has its own workOffset, never invented rotary motion.
- turn: profile (2-200 of {diameter,z}), stock {diameter,length}, stepdown, allowance, feedMmRev, rpm, machine, machineConfig. Z0 at face, negative towards chuck, X diameter. External rough/finish passes, stock profiles and G18/G94/G97 NC. The turning centre (machine preset id or machineConfig) bounds the bar and the spindle: a bar it cannot hold is refused. Volumes: stock = part + unmachined tail + removed. Ideal point-tool preview, no insert/chuck/controller qualification. Save via cam_edit turn. Mill-turn uses a B head and positioned C spindle.

Result (JSON)
- ok and data/findings. manufacture release/claims concern only modelled NC capabilities; turn always returns release preview. Neither qualifies a machine.

Do not use it
- For writes or stored verification: use cam_edit. For geometry: use cad.

After it
- Create/update with the checked revision, then generate, analyze full NC, verify and review evidence. Store turning via cam_edit turn; do not regenerate it with the legacy generator.
