Generate the G-code of an operation from its current settings and the drawing.

Use it
- After cam_create_operation or cam_update_operation, and whenever the geometry changed.

Do not use it
- To "look at" G-code you already have: cam_analyze_gcode takes text.

Parameters
- operationId: required in practice; without it the result is an error asking for it.
- maxLines: how many lines of the program to return as a preview, 1 to 200 (default 40).

Result (JSON)
- stats: lines, cutLength and rapidLength (mm), minZ, maxZ, estimatedSeconds, bounds.
- preview: the first maxLines lines. The whole program is stored on the operation and appears in the CAM page's editor and preview.
- {stats:{error}} when the operation is unknown or the generator refused the job (the message says why).

After it
- cam_analyze_gcode for static checks, then cam_verify_toolpath for the simulation. Report numbers with units; do not paste the whole program.
