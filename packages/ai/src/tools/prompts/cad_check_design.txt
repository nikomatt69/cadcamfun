Check the model in the drawing for what is illogical in 3D design, and get the design space it lives in (axes, planes, angle units, each solid's own axis).

Use it
- After building or changing a model with cad_add_elements / cad_modify_entities, before you tell the user it is done.
- When the user asks "is this right?", "does it fit?", "why is it floating?".
- Before sending a model to CAM: pass the stock so parts outside it are reported.

Do not use it
- For toolpaths or G-code (cam_analyze_gcode, cam_verify_toolpath). This reads the model only; it never changes it.

Parameters
- none. It reads the drawing the user has open, the drawing's tolerance and the stock of the project when there is one.

Result (JSON)
- ok: no error finding. issues: [{severity, code, elementId, message}], at most 50.
  Errors: NON_FINITE (NaN or infinite number), NON_POSITIVE_SIZE (a size <= 0), BAD_ROTATION, BOOLEAN_OPERANDS (fewer than two operands, or one that is not in the drawing).
  Warnings: SUSPECT_SCALE (over 20 m: a unit mixed up), BELOW_TOLERANCE (a size under the drawing tolerance), ROTATION_IN_RADIANS (rotation is DEGREES; 1.5708 is almost no turn), REVOLUTION_NEGATIVE_RADIUS, DUPLICATE (two identical overlapping copies), OUTSIDE_STOCK.
  Information: FLOATING (a solid touching no other solid; its x, y, z are probably wrong, remember they are its centre), COARSE_MESH (round shape drawn with too few segments for the tolerance).
- stats: elements, solids, bounds, settings (units, tolerance).
- designSpace: the conventions. Handedness right; Z is up; XY the ground plane; solids are placed by their centre; rotation in DEGREES, arc and revolution angles in RADIANS; cylinder, cone and torus stand along Z; cube width is X, height Y, depth Z.

Fix what it finds
- Move or resize with cad_modify_entities (center, width...), remove a duplicate with cad_delete_entities (only after telling the user), give round shapes a tolerance. Then check again.
