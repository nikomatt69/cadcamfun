Simulate an operation's G-code on its stock and report what it does to it: material removal, rapids through material, collisions of shank, holder and fixtures, cutting below the stock and, with a target part, gouges.

Use it
- After every cam_generate_gcode, before calling a program ready. Always.
- After every change that fixes something, to prove the fix.

Do not use it
- As a machine certification. A clean result means "no errors in what was modelled".

Parameters
- operationId: the operation to verify (its G-code is regenerated from its current settings).
- tool (optional): what you know about the cutter and holder, in mm: cuttingLength (flute length), shankDiameter, stickout (tip to holder face), holderDiameter, holderLength. A field you leave out is NOT checked; say so in your report.
- fixtures (optional): boxes the tool must not touch: [{id, bounds:{minX,maxX,minY,maxY,minZ,maxZ}}] in program coordinates (stock top is Z0).
- targetSolids (optional): the finished part as solids (Box, Cylinder, Sphere, Cone, Torus, Extrude, Revolve) in the same coordinates; enables gouge and uncut-stock results.
- stock (optional): {minX,maxX,minY,maxY,minZ,maxZ} when the operation has no stock; otherwise derived from the drawing and the operation.
- resolution (mm per cell) and tolerance (mm): leave out unless you need finer detail. Heights are exact; sideways, a wall is only known to within one cell. For micron checks narrow the stock to the region of interest (a few millimetres) and set resolution 0.002 to 0.005 and tolerance 0.001: the whole part at that resolution does not fit, and a large job is coarsened automatically (the result says so in ResolutionCoarsened).

Result (JSON)
- ok: true when there is no error finding. issues: [{code, severity, message, line, point, count}]. Codes: RapidInMaterial, ShankCollision, HolderCollision, FixtureCollision, BelowStock, Gouge (errors); UncutStock, ResolutionCoarsened, InterpreterWarning (information).
- stats: removedVolume, remainingVolume (mm3), engagedLength and airCutLength (mm), cell size, gougeVolume when a target was given.
- modelled: which of holder, shank, fixtures, target were checked. Quote it.
- The full report, with the remaining-stock map, is shown in the CAM page's Verify tab.

Errors with fixes
- RapidInMaterial: raise the retract / clearance height; ShankCollision: a longer cutter or less depth; HolderCollision: more stickout or less depth; FixtureCollision: move the job or the clamp; BelowStock: reduce the depth; Gouge: wrong offset side or depth. See the skill toolpath-verification.

Limits: three-axis height-field model, one tool per run, box fixtures. Say what was not modelled.
