Inspect the CAD drawing (elements, solids, assemblies, DFM); compute sketches, mates, sheets, meshes; read-only.

Actions and Parameters (lengths mm; history and joint angles degrees)
- describe: counts, bounds, layers; none; start here.
- check: sizes, operands, duplicates, floating parts; none.
- relations: firstId, secondId; bounds, placement, dependencies.
- grep: query, layerId?, parametricOnly?, includeParameters?, offset?, limit?; search properties, parameters, revisions.
- vision: focus?; capture the CAD viewport.
- inspect_elements: ids?, layerId?, offset?, limit?; full properties, paged.
- inspect_layers: none; layers, active layer.
- inspect_search: query, layerId?, limit?; matches.
- inspect_property: elementId, path (property-name array), offset?, maxChars?; one property, paged.
- inspect_origins: ids?, layerId?, offset?, limit?; origins, bounds, distance from zero.
- drawing: elementId or history, options?; dimensioned PDF/SVG; GDT, datums, balloons in options.
- parametric_validate: history; validate before storing.
- parametric_inspect: elementId; parameters and revision.
- parametric_test: elementId, cases (1-12); dimensional checks, variants.
- parametric_dfm: elementId or history, process (mill|turn|print), toolDiameter?, material? (metal|cast|plastic), minWall?, envelope?.
- parametric_exact: elementId or history, includeStep?, name?; exact body or why mesh-only.
- exact: ids?, machining?; exact validity, volume, bounds, faceNames, holes, CAM fit.
- assembly: washers?; clashes, gaps, BOM, joints, missing fasteners.
- setups: ids?, fixture?, allowed?; orientations per solid.
- title_block: none; the completed title block and what it lacks.
- distance: firstId, secondId; smallest exact gap and closest points.
- kernel: op health|refs|resolve, elementId or history, body?, kinds?, limit?, refs?, parameters?, after?, madeAfter?; stable refs (skill cad-naming), naming health, Boolean counters; resolve gives exact|split|merged|deleted|ambiguous.
- workflow: op sketch|assembly|sheet|mesh_import|mesh_export; entities, constraints?, drag?; definitions, occurrences, mates?, drive?, checks?; elementId or history, options?, format? pdf|svg|stl|obj|3mf; attachment or data, unit?, repair?, requireSolid?; computed, not stored; skill cad-workflow.
- shell: id, thickness, open? (face names), wallByFace? [{face,thickness}]; preview; names from exact.
- hole: id, hole|boss|fillet|nurbs, diameter?|fill?|radius?|distance?|angle?, taper?, mode? (move|offset|draft), direction?, neutral?, pull?, tolerance?; preview; indexes from exact.
- offset: id, distance; outward offset preview, rounded convex corners; unsupported says why.
- motion: model, sweep; scene part ids, zero-pose origins/axes. closesLoop solves passive joints. Reports closure, gaps, clearances per frame; no contact forces.
- surface: operation patch|stitch|thicken, patches (data, trim?), thickness?, direction? {x,y,z}, tolerance?, name?. points[u][v]=[x,y,z]. Isoparametric rectangular trim; stitch needs matching full boundaries; curved thicken needs an axial direction (other normal offsets refused); open surfaces are not CAM solids.
- exchange: format step|iges|parasolid, ids?; file text. Parasolid is unsupported.
- parametric_fastener: size?, fit?, shaftDiameter?; thread, tap, clearance, counterbore.
- parametric_recipe: recipe, params?; standard-part history. Recipes: mounting_plate, flange, l_bracket, stepped_shaft, pipe_bend, duct_transition, enclosure.
- parametric_from_element: elementId, depth?; named dimensions from geometry.

Paging: limit goes up to 500 on inspect_elements, inspect_origins and inspect_search (ids too; defaults 10, 10, 20) and up to 200 on grep (default 20).

Result (JSON)
ok, geometry findings or problems; paged inspection gives total/nextOffset. Element ids are edit ids; revisions guard later updates.

Do not use it
To write use cad_edit; to machine use cam/cam_edit. Consult fusion-360-modelling for history features.
