Move, turn or scale elements that already exist, or lay out copies of them (a row, a grid step, a circular pattern), by id. It does what the editor's gizmo does, so the result is where a person would have put it.

Use it
- "Move the plate 20 mm up", "turn the wheel 90 degrees", "make it twice as big", "four more of these 30 mm apart", "six holes on a circle".
- Rotating or scaling several parts together about one point (about): they keep their places relative to each other.

Do not use it
- To change a size or any other field (width, radius, color, layer): cad_modify_entities.
- To create something new from nothing: cad_add_elements.
- To copy a Boolean: copy its operands and build the Boolean again.

Parameters
- ids: 1 to 100 element ids, from cad_describe_drawing or an add result.
- dx, dy, dz: move by this much in mm along the world axes (Z up). Leave out what is 0.
- rotateAxis: "x", "y" or "z", the world axis to turn about (default "z"). rotateDegrees: the angle in DEGREES (positive is counter-clockwise looking down the axis).
- scale: a factor above 0 (2 = twice as big, 0.5 = half), the same on every axis. Keep it uniform.
- about: {x, y, z} the point to turn or scale about, in mm. Without it each element turns or scales about its own centre. Supported for solids, extrusions, revolutions, booleans, curves and components; for an arc, spring, bolt, screw, wall, stair, door or roof move it first and turn it about its own centre.
- copies: how many copies to make (0 = transform the originals). Copy k is the transform applied k times to the original, so dx: 30 with copies: 4 is a row of five, rotateDegrees: 60 with about and copies: 5 is a ring of six.
- Order when several are given: scale, then turn, then move.

Result (JSON)
- ok, mode ("transformed" or "copied"), ids (the elements that changed or the new copies), elements (what is stored now). The editor shows the change at once and the user can undo it.
- ok false with an error when an id is unknown, an element cannot be turned about a point, or nothing was asked for.
