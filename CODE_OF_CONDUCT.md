# Code of conduct

CADCAMFUN welcomes participants of every background and experience level.
Treat people with respect, discuss the work constructively, and allow others
to learn, disagree and correct mistakes without personal attacks.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project
discussions and other spaces operated by the project, and to people representing
the project in an official capacity.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, useful feedback and assume good faith when reasonable.
- Respect different experiences, accessibility needs and communication styles.
- Respect confidentiality, copyright and personal boundaries.
- Keep technical discussions focused and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexual harassment, impersonation,
doxxing, spam and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not disclose
private contact information, security reports or confidential models.

## Reports and moderation

Send a private message to Nicola Mattioli through
[@nikomatt69 on X](https://x.com/nikomatt69). If direct messages are unavailable,
ask for a private contact channel without publishing incident details. Share only
necessary information; the reporting platform has its own privacy practices.

The maintainer reviews available evidence and may remove content, request a
correction, issue a warning, restrict participation or ban an account. Measures
should reflect severity, repetition and risk. Reports are handled discreetly,
with information shared only as needed to investigate, protect participants or
meet legal obligations; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

You may request reconsideration privately with relevant additional information.
If a complaint concerns the maintainer or you cannot safely use the project
channel, use the hosting platform's abuse-reporting tools or an appropriate
independent authority. No retaliation against good-faith reporters is acceptable.

This is a participation policy, not an additional restriction on open-source
license rights. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
