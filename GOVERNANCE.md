# Project governance

CADCAMFUN is maintained by Nicola Mattioli
([@nikomatt69](https://x.com/nikomatt69)). The maintainer decides project scope,
reviews contributions, authorizes releases and administers project-owned spaces.
Additional maintainers may be appointed explicitly; a merged contribution does
not itself grant repository administration or release authority.

## Decisions

Use an issue for a concrete proposal with its motivation, compatibility impact,
alternatives and expected maintenance cost. Significant architectural decisions
belong in the relevant specification before implementation. Maintainers may
decline work that does not fit the product, licensing boundaries or maintenance
capacity and should explain consequential decisions when practical.

Disclose conflicts of interest. Reconsideration can be requested with new
evidence. Participation does not guarantee a vote, merge, feature, release or
paid role. [Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [security](SECURITY.md) concerns
have their own reporting processes.

## Rights and policy changes

Contributors retain rights in their work under the agreed contribution terms.
The maintainer does not acquire ownership or unrestricted relicensing rights
merely by accepting a pull request. Changes to the licensing of work owned by
others require the necessary authority. Existing irrevocable grants remain
effective under their terms.

Repository policy changes are recorded in version control. Participation rules
may evolve, but do not rewrite Apache-2.0, third-party rights, existing agreements
or mandatory law. The [root license](LICENSE) defines the licensing boundaries.
