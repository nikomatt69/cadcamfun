## Engineering verification

CAD, CAM, AI suggestions and simulation are engineering aids. They are not a
certificate of conformity, proof that a component is manufacturable, or approval
to operate a machine. Review units, dimensions, materials, tools, stock, fixtures,
machine configuration, postprocessor and controller requirements for the actual
job. A qualified operator must independently validate output and follow the
machine manufacturer's procedures before running NC or manufacturing a part.

AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Simulations and verification cover
only the conditions actually modeled and the checks actually implemented.
Retain backups and test changes with disposable data and appropriate safe
procedures. These statements do not exclude responsibility that mandatory law
does not allow to be excluded or modify a separate written warranty.
